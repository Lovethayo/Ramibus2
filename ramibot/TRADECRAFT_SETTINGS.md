# RAMIBUS Tradecraft settings

The former OSIRIS-specific configuration is being consolidated under **RAMIBUS Tradecraft**. Configure optional intelligence sources here after setup:

- aviation and OpenSky
- CCTV and public camera feeds
- scanner/recon backend
- sanctions, crypto, and phone-intelligence providers where legally authorized
- weather, fires, satellites, maritime, news, and conflict feeds
- private SearXNG routing and optional Tor routing
- future registered capabilities and MCP providers

The configuration is intentionally capability-based. A key only enables the documented provider or improves its rate limits; it does not automatically grant permission to scan, contact, modify, or publish anything.
